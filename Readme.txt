# 🤖 AI News Bot

AI News Bot is a simple automation tool that **collects news from RSS feeds**, summarizes the content, and posts it automatically to a **Telegram channel**.  
The project is designed for quick access to important news and automatic publishing.

---

## ✨ Features
- Fetch news from multiple RSS sources (Forex, Gold, Technology, etc.)
- Summarize the news text
- Save summarized news to `post_ready.txt`
- Auto-post to a Telegram channel
- Support scheduling for sending at a specific time
